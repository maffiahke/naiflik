Questions

Everything you wanted to ask

Is Naiflick really free to use?
Yes — completely. Creating a profile, browsing, matching and chatting with your matches costs nothing. There are no hidden fees, no paywalls and no subscriptions. Naiflick is built to be accessible to every Kenyan.
Is my personal information safe?
Absolutely. We never share your data with third parties, and your exact location is never shown to other members — only an approximate distance. You control what you share and can hide your profile at any time.
How do you verify profiles?
Every new member can complete a photo verification (a quick selfie pose match) and an optional national ID check. Verified profiles get a blue tick and are 3x more likely to get matches.
Does Naiflick work outside Nairobi?
Definitely. We have active members in all 47 counties, with the largest communities in Nairobi, Mombasa, Kisumu, Nakuru, Eldoret and Thika. You can filter by town or county to find people nearby.
How do I stay safe when meeting someone?
Always meet in a public place for the first few dates, tell a friend or family member where you're going, and keep your own transport arrangements. Our Safety Centre has more tips — and you can report or block anyone from their profile in one tap.
